# UX Principles and Tone Guide

## Target User

Women in Malaysia who want private, reliable personal health tracking without visual pressure or game-like engagement loops.

## Experience Pillars

1. Calm by default
   - Use low-stimulation layout and restrained motion.
   - Keep one primary action per screen state.
2. Private by design
   - Ask for the least sensitive data needed.
   - Surface privacy controls early and clearly.
3. Trustworthy in language
   - Avoid alarmist claims and avoid diagnosis-like wording.
   - Present confidence and uncertainty in simple terms.
4. Respectful and non-judgmental
   - No guilt framing for missed logs.
   - No engagement streaks or competitive mechanics.

## Visual Direction

- Palette: muted neutrals with one soft accent color.
- Contrast: WCAG-compliant text contrast in all states.
- Motion: subtle transitions under 200ms where needed for orientation.
- Density: generous spacing with readable typography for calm scanning.
- Never use flashy gradients, confetti, progress fireworks, or bright badges.

## Content Tone

- Voice: supportive, private, clinically careful.
- Sentence style: short and clear, no fear-inducing language.
- Examples:
  - Good: "Your next cycle estimate may adjust as more data is added."
  - Avoid: "Warning: your cycle is abnormal."
- Medical safety:
  - Include "informational, not medical advice" in risk-sensitive sections.
  - Provide clinician escalation copy for urgent symptoms.

## Localization and Regional Fit

- Date defaults for Malaysia.
- Language-ready content model with support for local-language labels.
- Neutral cultural framing for reproductive choices:
  - Trying to conceive
  - Pregnant
  - General cycle tracking

## Non-Goals

- No gamification:
  - no streaks
  - no points
  - no leaderboards
  - no reward animations
